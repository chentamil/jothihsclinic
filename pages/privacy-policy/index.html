Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [Insert Date]

Jothi Heart & Skin Clinic ("Jothi Heart & Skin Clinic", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store, protect, and otherwise process information when you visit https://www.jothiheartandskin.com/ ("Website"), contact us, submit an enquiry, or request an appointment through the Website.

By using this Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required under applicable law, we will seek appropriate consent for the relevant processing activity.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our Website and services, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Mobile or telephone number
  • Email address
  • Preferred appointment date and time
  • Age or date of birth, where required
  • Gender, where required for an appointment or service
  • Information provided in an enquiry or appointment request
  • Information necessary to respond to your request
  • Other information that you voluntarily provide to us

If you voluntarily provide information relating to your health, symptoms, previous treatment, medical history, skin condition, cardiac condition, or other medical circumstances, such information may constitute health-related information and will be handled with appropriate care and confidentiality.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our website infrastructure, hosting providers, security services, analytics tools, or other technologies.

This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website or page
  • General website usage and diagnostic information

This information may be used to maintain website security, understand website usage, troubleshoot technical issues, and improve the Website and user experience.

3. Appointment and Enquiry Information

When you submit an appointment request or enquiry through the Website, we may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry.
  • Contact you regarding your appointment request.
  • Confirm, reschedule, or cancel an appointment.
  • Provide information about our clinic and services.
  • Coordinate your requested consultation or service.

Submitting an online appointment request does not by itself guarantee an appointment. Appointment requests are subject to confirmation by the clinic.

4. Health and Medical Information

We recognise that health and medical information may be sensitive. Patients and website visitors should provide only information that is reasonably necessary for an enquiry or appointment request.

Please avoid submitting highly sensitive medical records, identity documents, financial information, passwords, or other confidential information through a general Website form unless the clinic specifically requests such information through an appropriate communication channel.

Medical information collected during a consultation or treatment may form part of the patient's medical or clinical records and may be retained and processed in accordance with applicable professional, legal, medical, and regulatory requirements.

5. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information for legitimate purposes related to operating our clinic and Website, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests.
  • Managing appointment requests.
  • Providing healthcare-related services.
  • Communicating with patients regarding appointments and services.
  • Maintaining appropriate administrative and patient records.
  • Improving our Website and user experience.
  • Maintaining Website security.
  • Preventing misuse, fraud, or unauthorised activity.
  • Handling complaints and service-related enquiries.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

6. Communication with Patients and Website Visitors

If you provide your telephone number, email address, or other contact information, we may use it to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about appointments or services that you have requested.

Depending on the communication methods used by the clinic, we may contact you through telephone calls, SMS, email, WhatsApp, or other available communication channels.

Where promotional or marketing communications require consent under applicable law, we will seek such consent as required.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for monetary gain.

Your information may be accessed or shared where reasonably necessary to provide healthcare or administrative services, operate the Website, communicate with you, maintain technical infrastructure, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights, safety, and security of the clinic, patients, and others.

Depending on the services used by the clinic, personal information may be processed by trusted service providers such as website hosting providers, website maintenance providers, email providers, communication platforms, appointment systems, analytics providers, security providers, or other technology service providers.

Such service providers may process information as necessary to provide the services for which they have been engaged, subject to applicable contractual, legal, and privacy requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

Our Website may use or display services provided by third parties, including Google Maps, Google services, WhatsApp, social media platforms, analytics services, security services, or other external technologies.

These third-party services may collect or process information according to their own terms, privacy policies, and practices.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services before using them or providing information through them.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Website may use cookies and similar technologies to support Website functionality, security, analytics, and user experience.

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device by a website and can help the Website recognise your browser or remember certain information.

You may be able to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some Website functionality.

10. Google Services and Analytics

Our Website may use Google services such as Google Maps, Google Analytics, Google reCAPTCHA, or other Google technologies.

These services may collect technical or usage information in accordance with their respective privacy policies and terms.

Where applicable, information collected through these services may be used to help maintain Website security, understand Website usage, prevent abuse, and improve our online services.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled by the clinic to help protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Our Website may use HTTPS/SSL technology to help protect information transmitted between your browser and the Website.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should take reasonable precautions when using the Internet and avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through general Website forms.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, maintaining appropriate records, resolving disputes, complying with legal obligations, and protecting our legitimate interests.

Medical and patient records may be retained for periods required by applicable professional, legal, or regulatory requirements.

13. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to or correction of certain information held by us.

If you believe that personal information provided by you is inaccurate or incomplete, you may contact us and request that the information be reviewed or corrected, subject to applicable legal and medical-record requirements.

14. Children's Privacy

This Website is not intended to encourage children to independently submit personal information online.

Where healthcare services are provided to a minor, personal information may be collected and processed in accordance with applicable law and the clinic's procedures, including involvement of a parent or legal guardian where required.

15. Links to Other Websites

Our Website may contain links to websites, applications, or services operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of external websites or services. You should review the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Website, services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us.
