Microneedling

At Jothi Heart & Skin Clinic , Microneedling, also known as Collagen Induction Therapy (CIT), uses a device with fine needles to create controlled micro-injuries on the skin surface. This triggers the skin's natural healing response, stimulating collagen and elastin production — improving skin texture, firmness, and overall appearance over time. It is highly effective for acne scars, open pores, stretch marks, fine lines, and uneven skin texture. Performed by Dr. R. Sivayogana, MD DVL · DNB DVL. A mild redness lasting 24–48 hours is normal after the procedure.

Multiple sessions are recommended for optimal results, typically 3–6 sessions spaced 4 weeks apart. Available at Jothi Heart & Skin Clinic, Kanchipuram.

- Dr. R. Sivayogana, MD DVL · DNB DVL
