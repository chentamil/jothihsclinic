About Dr. D. Suresh

Dr. D. Suresh is a Consultant Interventional Cardiologist with a special interest in interventional cardiology, coronary interventions and coronary angioplasty.

He completed his Higher Secondary education at Mamallan Matriculation Higher Secondary School, Kanchipuram. During his Higher Secondary education, he secured the Second Rank in Kanchipuram District and was among the Top State Rank Holders in Tamil Nadu, securing the 8th Rank in the state in 2007.

He subsequently pursued his undergraduate medical education (MBBS) at the prestigious Madras Medical College through merit and graduated in 2013, with distinction in General Medicine and Pathology.

Following his undergraduate medical education, he joined the Tamil Nadu Government Medical Service and later pursued postgraduate training in MD (General Medicine) at Stanley Medical College, Chennai. He completed his postgraduate course in 2020 with distinction.

Driven by his interest in cardiology, he pursued super-specialty training in DM (Cardiology) at Madras Medical College, Chennai, completing the programme in 2024.

His field of interest is Interventional Cardiology, with particular interest in coronary interventions and coronary angioplasty.
