GFC Hair Therapy

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) is an advanced evolution of PRP therapy that uses a highly concentrated preparation of growth factors derived from your own blood. Unlike standard PRP, GFC eliminates red blood cells and white blood cells from the preparation — delivering a purer, more potent concentration of growth factors directly to the scalp.

This results in more effective hair follicle stimulation, reduced hair fall, and stronger, denser hair growth. Performed by Dr. R. Sivayogana, MD DVL · DNB DVL. 6 sessions recommended, spaced 3–4 weeks apart. Non-surgical, safe, and natural.

GFC Hair Treatment in Kanchipuram

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) hair treatment is a non-surgical procedure designed to help reduce hair fall and improve hair growth using the patient’s own growth factors. The treatment supports weak hair follicles, improves scalp circulation, and promotes healthier, thicker hair growth naturally. At Jothi Heart & Skin Clinic, Kanchipuram, GFC therapy is performed after scalp evaluation and hair loss assessment by experienced dermatology professionals.

What is GFC Hair Treatment?

GFC therapy is an advanced regenerative treatment used for hair thinning, early baldness, and excessive hair fall. A small quantity of the patient’s blood is collected and processed to isolate concentrated growth factors. These growth factors are then injected into the scalp to stimulate inactive hair follicles and improve hair density.

How GFC Therapy Helps Hair Growth

The concentrated growth factors help nourish the scalp and strengthen hair roots. The treatment supports natural hair regeneration by improving blood supply around follicles and encouraging healthier hair growth cycles. Many patients notice reduced hair shedding and improvement in hair texture over multiple sessions.

Conditions Commonly Treated with GFC

  • Male pattern hair loss
  • Female pattern hair thinning
  • Early-stage baldness
  • Hair fall after stress or illness
  • Weak and thinning hair
  • Reduced hair density

Who Can Benefit from GFC Treatment?

GFC hair treatment is generally suitable for individuals experiencing mild to moderate hair thinning or ongoing hair fall. It is most effective when hair follicles are still active. Patients with early hair loss often achieve better outcomes compared to completely bald areas where follicles are inactive.

Procedure for GFC Hair Therapy

Step Description
Consultation Hair and scalp evaluation by the doctor.
Blood Collection A small amount of blood is collected from the patient.
Growth Factor Separation The blood sample is processed to extract concentrated growth factors.
Scalp Injection The prepared GFC solution is injected into targeted scalp areas.
Recovery Patients can usually return to routine activities on the same day.

Benefits of GFC Hair Treatment

  • Non-surgical and minimally invasive procedure
  • Uses the patient’s own growth factors
  • Helps reduce hair fall
  • Improves scalp nourishment
  • Supports healthier and thicker hair growth
  • Minimal downtime after treatment

How Many Sessions Are Needed?

The number of sessions varies depending on the severity of hair loss and the patient’s scalp condition. Many patients undergo multiple sessions spaced several weeks apart for better and longer-lasting results. Maintenance sessions may also be recommended periodically.

Aftercare Following GFC Therapy

  • Avoid washing the scalp for at least 24 hours
  • Avoid direct sun exposure immediately after treatment
  • Follow the prescribed scalp care routine
  • Avoid harsh chemical hair products for a few days
  • Attend follow-up sessions as advised

Possible Side Effects

Mild redness, swelling, or temporary discomfort at the injection sites may occur after the procedure. These effects are usually short-lived and settle within a few days. Since the treatment uses the patient’s own blood components, allergic reactions are uncommon.
