Clinical Cardiac Evaluation

Clinical Cardiac Evaluation is the first and most essential step in understanding a patient’s heart health. At Jothi Heart & Skin Clinic , we offer a thorough and personalised evaluation to detect cardiovascular conditions early and accurately. This process involves a detailed discussion of your symptoms, medical history, lifestyle habits, and family history of cardiac diseases. Our doctors and staffs carefully assesses your blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, and other vital signs.

The evaluation includes a physical examination where heart sounds, rhythm, and pulse characteristics are observed for any abnormalities. We also focus on identifying risk factors like hypertension, diabetes, cholesterol levels, obesity, and stress that could contribute to heart disease.

Depending on the findings, further diagnostic tests such as ECG, echocardiogram, Chest X Ray, treadmill test if applicable, appropriate blood investigations may be recommended to support the clinical diagnosis.

Clinical cardiac evaluation is crucial for early recognition of coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, cardiomyopathy, valve disorders, or heart failure. For patients with no known history of heart problems, this evaluation serves as an excellent preventive tool, especially for those over 40 or with sedentary lifestyles.

At Sri Raju Hospital, our cardiology team combines expertise, compassion, and advanced tools to deliver precise clinical evaluations that form the foundation of every successful treatment journey. We ensure you leave with clarity, guidance, and a heart-healthy path forward.
